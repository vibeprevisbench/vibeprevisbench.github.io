The benchmark

Can a coding agent turn a script, a few photos, a first frame or real footage into an executable 4D scene that a director can use?

Each task hands the agent a brief and Blender. It writes and runs code, renders previews, inspects its own frames, and delivers a greybox scene: who stands where, who moves when, how every shot is framed and where it cuts. We check the exported scene fact by fact against hidden ground truth, and a VLM judge watches the rendered proxy.

    VLM judge robustness

    Would another judge rank them differently?

    We re-judged every video with GPT-6.1 Sol and Qwen3.8 Omni Flash using the same questions, and compared all judges with human pairwise preferences. The judges differ in absolute level, but they rank the agents almost identically, and each tracks human judgement closely.
